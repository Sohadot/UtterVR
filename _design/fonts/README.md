These fonts are used only to render `assets/uttervr-social.jpg` from
`_design/social-card.html`. They are not loaded by the live page.

| `mono.woff2`   | IBM Plex Mono, 500          | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | `OFL-IBMPlexMono.txt`  |

Licence texts are copied from the corresponding `ofl/*/OFL.txt` in
https://github.com/google/fonts.
